About

Portuguese-Luxembourgish, engineering things that grip, turn, and hold.

I grew up between Penafiel, Portugal and the Luxembourg countryside, which is probably why I gravitate toward systems with a lot of moving parts. I'm finishing my Bachelor's at KU Leuven Group T in September 2026 and continuing straight into the Master's in Electromechanical Engineering Technology, specializing in mechatronics with a focus on advanced robotics — my last year at Group T. Selected Work

What I've been building.

Olympus MARS — Robotic Arm Subsystem

Co-founded Olympus MARS, KU Leuven's student Mars rover team, in March 2026. I lead the robotic arm subsystem heading into the European Rover Challenge 2027 — requirements, arm-vs-CNC tradeoff studies, and a growing team of engineers — and serve as Director of Finance & Operations for the club.

Team Lead ERC 2027 20-Person Team

Anomaly Detection for Robotic Unscrewing

My thesis builds an unsupervised anomaly-detection pipeline for a robot that unscrews hard drives — catching bad screws and misalignment from torque-sensor data on the arm's T2 and T3 joints. kNN is running; Isolation Forest and an autoencoder are next, benchmarked against it.

Python ML Sensor Data

Designed, built and shipped the Olympus MARS team website from scratch — HTML/CSS, domain registration, and hosting — because someone had to and I didn't mind.
