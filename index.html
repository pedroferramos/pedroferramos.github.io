Basic care

What each plant needs to keep rooting well after it leaves here.

Light

Bright, indirect light for all four — a spot near a window but out of direct midday sun. Calathea tolerates lower light best.

Water

Let the top few cm of soil dry between waterings. Cuttings still in water: refresh weekly until roots reach 3–4cm, then pot up.

First weeks

Keep newly potted cuttings out of direct sun and drafts for the first two weeks while roots settle in.
