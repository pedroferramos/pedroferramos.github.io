Boom Burgers & co.

My First Enterprise, by Jonk Entrepreneuren Luxembourg · 2020 · 1st Prize

Together with three friends, I co-founded a burger stand as part of My First Enterprise, a 10-week school entrepreneurship program run by Jonk Entrepreneuren Luxembourg (JEL), where teams start a real business from scratch. We handled everything ourselves: the concept, branding, sourcing, pricing, and running the stand. We ended up winning 1st place.
