Lux'OR

Independent venture · 2022

In my last year of high school, I co-founded Lux'OR with two friends: a peach-flavored liqueur, made independently rather than through a school program. We partnered with Difrulux, a local distillery based in Consdorf, Luxembourg, who produced the liqueur for us. We visited the distillery to taste-test the recipe and choose the bottles and cork, and designed the labels ourselves before having them printed.

We sold bottles to friends and to the restaurant that hosted our school's gala dinner, where it was served during the celebration. Once we all left for university in different places, the project wound down.
