Olympus MARS

Co-founder & Robotic Arm Subsystem Lead · KU Leuven Student Rover Team

I co-founded Olympus MARS in March 2026, KU Leuven's student rover team competing in the European Rover Challenge (ERC), an international competition where student teams design and build Mars rover prototypes. I lead the robotic arm subsystem, working alongside a growing team to take it from early requirements and design tradeoffs through to a finished build for ERC 2027. I also serve as Director of Finance and Operations, handling budgeting and internal operations, and I built and deployed the team's website.

The team has grown to around 20 members, and the arm subsystem is still in its early research phase, working toward ERC 2027.
