3DOF Robotic Arm for Vertical Farming

3rd-Year Bachelor Project · Group of 4

Designed a 3-degree-of-freedom industrial robotic arm for a tomato vertical farm as part of a 3rd-year Bachelor project. Built a digital twin in Siemens NX and programmed inverse kinematics in TIA Portal to compensate for tilt, since the arm sits on an AGV that can't hold a horizontal position.
